01Body-in-white

The shell lifts off in one piece.

The E30 has no separate chassis frame. Floor, sills, pillars and roof are spot-welded into a single load-bearing unibody, then primed, painted and sealed under clear-coat.

Lift it off and very little is left underneath: two subframes carry the suspension, and the engine, gearbox, exhaust and fuel tank hang between them.

02Engine

The M20 straight-six, exploded.

BMW's small six: a cast-iron block, an aluminium head and a single overhead camshaft turned by a toothed rubber belt. Two valves per cylinder make in all. In the E30 it sits canted to the right so it fits under the low bonnet.

Live: the crank is turning, the cam follows at half speed, and each valve opens on time for firing order .

03Drivetrain

Clutch, gearbox, driveshaft, differential.

Torque leaves the flywheel through a single dry-plate clutch, passes through a five-speed, runs down a two-piece driveshaft with a rubber flex disc and a centre bearing, and turns 90° in the differential.

Gear sizes in the cutaway follow the ratios. Multiply by the :1 final drive to get the overall ratio at the wheels.

04Suspension

Struts at the front, semi-trailing arms at the back.

MacPherson struts locate the front wheels. At the rear, each wheel hangs on an arm that pivots about an axis swept from square, so the wheel tilts inward as it rises.

Scrolling drives the wheels through bump and droop. The camber and toe readouts are calculated from the pivot geometry on every frame, not taken from a table.

05Power flow

Fuel and spark in, torque out, and back again.

At full throttle, fuel and spark make torque at the crank. The gearbox and final drive multiply it on its way to the rear tyres.

Lift off and the flow reverses. The wheels now drive the engine, the cuts the injectors, and engine braking slows the car. Readouts use the real gearing and tyre size. The 3D motion runs in slow motion.

06Model years

Pick a year and the car rebuilds.

Cars up to 1987 have slim chrome bumpers and chrome trim. From 1988 they get polyurethane bumpers, black trim and smoked tail lamps. The engine, gearing, tyres and every figure follow the year too.

    07Spec sheet

    Every number on this page comes from here.

    The page reads one spec object for the selected year and derives the rest from it: displacement from bore and stroke, road speed from gearing and tyre size, wheel torque from the torque curve and the ratios, and camber change from the suspension pivots.

    Built procedurally in Three.js from code. There are no model files: every panel, bore, gear and spring is generated from the spec model at load and again whenever the year changes.
